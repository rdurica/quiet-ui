# Misc.: chat, quests, highlights

Modern chat, quest update notices, Highlights, Quest mobs, and Parchment windows.

- Chat keeps its text and drops the chrome while Modern chat is on (the default). Each line is a translucent black bubble; a new line slides in from the left and older lines ease upward. At the bottom, a line fades across its last half second and drops after the fade interval (10 s unless setup says otherwise; 0 keeps it). The lines stay in the list, so scrolling still walks the full history. Hovering the chat block shows the lines that fit in the window, including ones that already faded, and the wheel works on that whole block. Leaving it fades back to the bottom view. Hover a line to copy it. A numbered channel shows only its number, and guild, party, raid and the other group tags use a short letter. Joining, leaving, or changing a channel is not shown. Money and experience follow the chat tab filters (Experience and Currency). Loot stays. The input is a flat field, visible at once while focused, and it fades out when it loses focus. Enter stays a Blizzard binding. Turning Modern chat off leaves the original chat untouched.
- Quest acceptance, objective progress, and completion briefly show only the changed quest as tracker text, the first item in the quest area, with no background or border. Acceptance includes its goals; progress includes only changed goals; completion says Complete. The notice stays for 5 s and fades over 0.3 s. Up to five quests queue, including the current one; repeated changes merge and refresh that quest. Login, reload, enabling, abandonment, and turn-in are silent. While Quest updates is on, WoW's center-screen quest acceptance, progress and completion text is not shown. Experience, money, quest failures and other errors stay. Hover anywhere on the quest area dismisses the visible notice at once, and moving away does not bring it back. A later queued quest can still show after the mouse leaves. Glance, Edit Mode, Always visible, and Only on hover keep suppressing notices while their time continues. Misc. holds Quest updates under the chat choices (`questNotice`; missing means on, `false` turns notices off) and Text size (`questNoticeSize`: Smaller, Default, or Larger; missing means Default, the tracker size; Larger is a little bigger and Smaller is only a little smaller). The Quests info button describes what the notice shows.
- Highlights are off unless Misc. checks Herbs, Ore, or Interact objects (`highlights`; shared through presets, Reset clears it). The object the game picks for the Interact key (soft interact) gets a softly pulsing glow with three four-point glints and four small motes that slowly rise and fade for every recognized category, without an expanding ring, on its nameplate: herbs muted green (`0.30, 0.74, 0.40`), ore gold (`0.95, 0.75, 0.25`), interact objects warm white (`0.78, 0.76, 0.70`). The category comes from the `SoftTargetFrame` icon texture; an unknown icon shows nothing. Interact objects matches `Cursor Crosshair_Interact_64` and its out-of-reach variant `Cursor Crosshair_UnableInteract_64`, the generic gear icon, including non-quest interactions; it does not identify quest relevance. Between the Highlights title and the checks, one line says Highlights turn on soft targeting for the Interact key, so nearby NPCs show their names (the client draws those names; QuietUI cannot hide them). The saved `highlights.quest` key is retained for compatibility with characters and presets. While active, QuietUI sets `SoftTargetInteract` 3, `SoftTargetInteractRange` 15, `SoftTargetNameplateInteract` 1, `SoftTargetIconGameObject` 1, `SoftTargetInteractArc` 2 (selection without directly facing the object), and `SoftTargetIconInteract` 1 (the nameplate and icon CVars only while the soft target has a `GameObject` GUID; otherwise the nameplate keeps its original value and the icon its original value, or 0 when the original `SoftTargetInteract` was 0, so NPCs get no plate, name, or icon), keeps the originals in `QuietUIDB.highlightCVars`, and writes them back when Highlights turn off, QuietUI turns off, or an instance begins; a change in combat waits for combat to end, even after `/quiet off`. Highlights remain active during combat, including target and nameplate changes; CVars are still changed or restored only out of combat. Instances (party, raid, pvp, arena) suppress Highlights. The highlighted object's `SoftTargetFrame.Icon` is held at alpha 0 while its texture remains readable for category detection; its saved alpha is restored when the highlight ends. A soft target in an unchecked or unknown category also has its icon held at alpha 0 when the saved original made that icon hidden: `SoftTargetIconInteract` 0 for any target, or `SoftTargetIconGameObject` 0 for a `GameObject` GUID. When the original showed it, or the CVars are not changed yet, its icon is left alone. The glow is parented to the plate and never changes the plate's alpha or points; it appears and hides at once (no 0.3 s fade) and returns to `UIParent` when hidden. Events only, no per-frame update. Glance, Edit Mode, and the HUD rules do not affect it.
- Quest mobs is on unless Misc. unchecks it (`questMobs`; shared through presets, Reset turns it back on). A small icon, about the health bar's height (at least 14 px), sits 6 px left of the health bar on the nameplate of an attackable mob (`UnitCanAttack`) that `C_QuestLog.UnitIsRelatedToActiveQuest` reports: a flat exclamation mark in the highlight colour (`Media/quest-kill.tga`) for a mob to kill, an illustrated leather pouch with a gold coin (`Media/quest-loot.tga`, its texture 12 % larger than the icon frame) when every open objective it serves is a quest item. The type comes from `C_TooltipInfo.GetUnit` lines (type 17 quest title with its ID, type 8 objective text) matched against `C_QuestLog.GetQuestObjectives` without counts or colon; a line at its goal (x/y with x >= y) or a finished objective is skipped; anything unmatched shows the exclamation. At most one icon per plate. Never `GameTooltip`. A secret or missing attack or quest-relation result shows nothing; unreadable tooltip data shows the exclamation. It is evaluated on `NAME_PLATE_UNIT_ADDED`, and quest log changes (`QUEST_LOG_UPDATE`, `UNIT_QUEST_LOG_CHANGED` for the player, `PLAYER_ENTERING_WORLD`) fold into one recalculation on the next frame; no per-frame update. `NAME_PLATE_UNIT_REMOVED` hides the icon and returns it to `UIParent`. The icon is parented to the plate, never takes the mouse, and never changes the plate's alpha or points. It shows in combat and instances; Glance, Edit Mode and the HUD rules do not affect it. Turning QuietUI or the option off hides every icon at once.
- Parchment windows is on unless Misc. unchecks it (`parchment`; shared through presets, Reset turns it back on). Quests from an NPC (`QuestFrame`: detail, progress, reward, greeting), NPC gossip (`GossipFrame`), and item text such as books, letters and signs (`ItemTextFrame`) show as clean parchment with softly torn edges (`Media/parchment.tga`), without the frame, portrait, side ornaments or decorations. The parchment is a warm, aged tone and reaches 12 px past each window edge. Text, objectives and rewards stay as they are; window size and position do not change. The window title is drawn in dark ink (`0.24, 0.16, 0.09`) in the same face, 2 px larger, so it reads on parchment; its text never changes. The buttons stay Blizzard buttons with their scripts and text, but sit flat in a bottom band, inset 14 px from the sides, their centre 15 px above the window bottom, level with CzechForever's CZ/EN row (arrow buttons sit on the bottom edge): the primary action (Accept, Complete, Continue, Next page) dark red, the secondary one (Decline, Goodbye, Prev page) muted grey; hover lightens the fill and a disabled button dims it. The close X becomes a small dark cross in the top right corner; Escape still closes. The text area ends above the buttons: the bottom anchors of the scroll frames (`QuestDetailScrollFrame`, `QuestProgressScrollFrame`, `QuestRewardScrollFrame`, `QuestGreetingScrollFrame`, `GossipFrame.GreetingPanel.ScrollBox`, `ItemTextScrollFrame`) are lifted so the text stops at least 4 px above the top of the button band; a scroll frame already high enough is untouched, and the lift is computed once from the original points so reapplying never lifts twice. The scroll bar track is dimmed, the thumb stays. Frame textures are held at alpha 0 through an own hook only while the option is on; original alphas, button points and sizes, scroll frame points and the title's font and colour are saved and restored exactly. The reskin reapplies on each window's `OnShow` and on `QUEST_DETAIL`, `QUEST_PROGRESS`, `QUEST_COMPLETE`, `QUEST_GREETING`, `GOSSIP_SHOW` and `ITEM_TEXT_READY`, and a load-on-demand window is picked up on `ADDON_LOADED`. Combat, instances, Glance, Edit Mode and the HUD rules do not affect it. Turning the option or QuietUI off restores the original look at once, including windows that are open. The quest log and map quest detail are out of scope.
