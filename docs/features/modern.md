# Modern: Modernize

The Modern tab of `/quiet setup`. Modernize gives Blizzard's windows and frames a quiet dark look by skinning them in place. Skins change textures, colours, TexCoords, points of windows and their own child regions; fade rules (bars, player frame, XP bar, auras, party) stay alpha-only and are untouched, and a skin never hides a frame with `Hide()`, `Show()` or a state driver.

Modernize skins action bars, unit frames, windows, map, minimap, tooltips, auras and XP/reputation. Each area can be enabled independently. Client frame variants are resolved at run time; missing parts are skipped. The actual look and client compatibility are checked in game.

## Settings

- Modernize is off unless the Modern tab checks it (`modernize.enabled`; missing means off). It is shared through presets and Reset default turns it off.
- While Modernize is checked the tab shows Theme, Background, Class colours and one checkbox per area. Unchecked, those options are hidden and keep their values. Nothing is stored until Save; closing the window discards the draft.
- Theme steps through the known themes. Today there is one: Dark (`modernize.theme`; missing or an unknown name means Dark).
- Background is the background alpha of skinned panels: 50, 75 or 90 percent (`modernize.opacity`; missing or any other value means 75). The stepper stops at both ends.
- Class colours is on unless unchecked (`modernize.classColors`; `false` turns it off). Areas that tint with the class colour read it.
- Area checkboxes follow the area registry in registration order, with each area's English label, in the right column. An area is on unless unchecked (`modernize.<area key>`; `false` turns that area off). Saving stores only values that differ from the defaults.
- The XP area (`status`, label Modern XP bar) heads its own XP and reputation section in the left column, under Reset positions. Under it, Show on big XP gain is on unless unchecked (`modernize.xpGain`; `false` turns it off) and is disabled while Modern XP bar is unchecked. While Modernize, the XP area and Show on big XP gain are on, quest XP and any single gain of at least 5 % of the level show the XP bar for 5 s (a further big gain extends it), even when the XP row is Only on hover. Mob-sized gains, a lower value after a level up and secret values do not; see [visible.md](visible.md).

## Theme

`dark` is background `0.05, 0.05, 0.05` with the chosen background alpha, opaque border `0.33, 0.33, 0.33, 1` (a translucent one is tinted by the world behind a translucent fill), accent `0.95, 0.75, 0.25` and the flat bar texture `Interface\Buttons\WHITE8X8`. A theme is one entry of data; `ns.Modern.Theme()` returns a copy of the active theme with the background alpha applied.

## Areas

An area is one file under `Features/Modern/Areas/` that calls `ns.Modern.Register{ key, label, apply, restore, events, onEvent, addons }`. A bad entry (missing key, label or apply, a reserved or duplicate key) is reported once and skipped.

- Apply: when Modernize and the area are on, `apply` runs once per area. Unchecking an area, or Modernize, restores only what changed. Changing the theme, background or class colours restores and applies the applied areas again.
- Restore: `/quiet off` or Reset restores every applied area in reverse registration order. Each area has a journal (`skin`) of what it changed; restore undoes it in reverse, so one area never touches another's frames, and held art alpha is released.
- Events: the events an area lists reach its `onEvent` only while the area is applied. Each event is registered on its own and a client that does not know it is skipped. `ADDON_LOADED` for an addon the area lists runs its `apply` again, so load-on-demand frames get skinned.
- Skin helpers: `Icon` (crop `0.08, 0.92, 0.08, 0.92`), `Border` and `Panel` (own regions, hidden on undo and reused), `Button` (flat fill, original art held hidden), `Hold` (art held at alpha 0, or a given alpha, against Blizzard through the alpha hook), and `Set` (any other `SetX` with its previous `GetX` value). A missing frame is skipped; a missing method is reported once.

## Movable windows

`ns.Modern.MakeMovable(frame, key)` adds an invisible drag handle over the top strip of a window. Dragging saves `windowPoints[key]` as `{ point, relativePoint, x, y }` relative to `UIParent` in the character settings (not in presets; Reset default keeps it). The saved point is put back on every show and after Blizzard positions its panels. In combat no point is set, no drag starts, and the wanted point is applied when combat ends. A window without a saved point keeps Blizzard's position. `ns.Modern.ReleaseMovable(frame)` stops the handle and returns to Blizzard's position; saved points are kept. The window's scripts are never replaced: one `HookScript("OnShow")` per window re-applies the saved point and does nothing once released; Reset positions re-anchors only windows that are still movable.

## Action bars

Apply: square-crop standard, multibar, pet, stance and gamepad action icons, replace button art with flat theme textures, add a thin border and outline hotkey/count text. Hold Blizzard bar decorations and slot art hidden: on 12.0 the `MainActionBar` `BorderArt` and the `EndCaps` (`LeftEndCap`, `RightEndCap`; the classic `MainMenuBar` textures as fallback), and per button `SlotArt`, `SlotBackground`, `NormalTexture` and `Border`. The 12.0 `IconMask` is taken off the icon so the square crop is not clipped. Keep the icon crop, button size, Edit Mode layout, bar alpha and combat behavior. Late gamepad loading receives the same skin through `ADDON_LOADED`.

State feedback: because Blizzard's hover, pressed and checked textures are held hidden, each button gets its own flat theme-coloured regions over the icon: a light hover region in the `HIGHLIGHT` layer, a dark one while pressed (`SetButtonState("PUSHED")` or mouse down) and an accent one while checked (`SetChecked`). They follow Blizzard through `hooksecurefunc` on `SetButtonState` and `SetChecked` and `HookScript` on `OnMouseDown` / `OnMouseUp`; no script is replaced, no field is written on the secure button, and nothing is shown or hidden on a bar. A secret checked state (12.0) is passed to the checked region with `SetAlphaFromBoolean` and never tested; a secret button state counts as not pushed.

Restore: release held art and restore button textures, crops, icon masks, fonts and colours; hide reusable own regions (including the state feedback). The hooks stay but do nothing while the area is off. The Bars feature exposes its buttons through `ns.Bars.Buttons()`.

## Unit frames

Apply: skin player, pet, target, target of target and focus in place. The retail 12.0 structure (`PlayerFrameContainer`, `TargetFrameContainer`, their content parts, `PetFrame` globals) is resolved at run time with the older flat names as fallbacks; missing or forbidden parts are skipped quietly.
- Frame art (frame texture, alternate power and vehicle textures, combat flash, resting glow `StatusTexture`, boss portrait frame, reputation colour strip) is held at alpha 0 against Blizzard's `SetAlpha`.
- Health and power bars get the theme flat texture, a themed background and a 1 px border. Blizzard retexturing a bar (`SetStatusBarTexture`) puts the theme texture back. The fill gets depth from three own textures one sublevel above it that follow the fill (the empty part stays a flat track): a white light on its top half fading from 22 % at the top to 0, a black shade over its whole height fading from 35 % at the bottom to 0, and, on health bars only, a 1 px white line at 18 % just under the top border (on the thin power bar light and line read as a stripe). A client without `SetGradient` / `CreateColor` keeps only a flat 8 % light on the top half.
- Health colour: player units use their class colour and NPCs their reaction colour (class colours on); class colours off, or a secret `UnitIsPlayer` / `UnitClass` / `UnitReaction` result (never used as a branch or table key), give a flat green, because the flat texture would otherwise be white. The colour is reapplied after every Blizzard `SetStatusBarColor` / `SetStatusBarTexture` and on target and focus changes and on faction and classification events for the skinned units (events for nameplates or party members do no work).
- Power colour follows the power type (`PowerBarColor`); mana is a softer blue (0.2, 0.45, 0.9) instead of Blizzard's pure blue. It is reapplied after `UnitFrameManaBar_UpdateType` (hooked with `hooksecurefunc`, no script replacement).
- Both bar fills are drawn at 80 % of that colour (each channel times 0.8), so the white value text stands out on light colours (mage cyan, rogue and energy yellow, priest white). The target and focus box keeps the full colour.
- Pet and target of target have no portraits, nor do target and focus while they show a player: those portraits are held at alpha 0 in place (points and size unchanged), also against 12.0 `SetAlphaFromBoolean`, which bypasses `SetAlpha` (every alpha hold re-applies its value after it). On a texture the vertex colour's alpha is its alpha, and Blizzard sets the target of target portrait's vertex colour every frame, so every alpha hold on a texture also re-applies its value after `SetVertexColor`. The target's health bar border carries its rarity: gold for elite, silver for rare/rareelite, red for bosses; every other border is themed.
- Player: the portrait stays visible, square where the level box would be (as tall as power bottom to the top of the name strip, at the frame's left portrait edge), with its round mask removed and a slight crop, 1 px inside an own rim in the theme border colour. The level text is a small number in the name's font (face and size; Blizzard's flags kept) 3 px inside the left end of the name strip, the name left-aligned 3 px right of it and ending 3 px before the strip's right end, and the level ring art is held at alpha 0. The combat icon (`PlayerFrameContent.PlayerFrameContentContextual.AttackIcon`, classic `PlayerAttackIcon`) is resized to 45 % of the portrait's side and sits 1 px inside its bottom-left corner. Blizzard's own fonts are read once per apply, so applying again does not shrink them further.
- The bars take the portrait's space. Target and focus get a square as tall as power bottom to the top of the name strip at the portrait's outer (right) edge. A player unit shows its class icon in it (the cell of `CLASS_ICON_TCOORDS` on Blizzard's character-create class sheet, trimmed 0.02 at each edge, 1 px inside the box) on a fill in its health colour (class colour, flat green per the rules above), so a 1 px rim of that colour shows. An NPC, a secret identity or a client without `CLASS_ICON_TCOORDS` shows its portrait instead: released from the hold, unmasked, cropped like the player's and anchored 1 px inside the box, which is filled with the theme border colour; a target turning into a player holds it again. It is repainted on every apply (target and focus changes, faction and classification events). The target's high-level skull is centred in it. Their level text is a small number in the name's font 3 px inside the left end of the name strip, the name left-aligned 3 px right of it up to 3 px before the strip's end, and the textures anchored to the level text (its ring art) are held at alpha 0, the same as the player's; QuietUI's own box and name strip are never taken for ring art, also when laying out again. The health bar keeps its own top and bottom; the power bar hangs from it with its top border on the health bar's bottom border (one shared 1 px line, no gap) and is at least 16 px tall so its 12 pt value text fits; the square reaches down to its new bottom. Health and power bars share the health bar's inner edge (Blizzard's target power bar is a little wider), reach to 2 px from the portrait or box, or to the portrait's outer edge for pet and target of target. Pet and target of target have no level: their name is left-aligned and spans the name strip, 3 px inside each end. Every unit's name strip is drawn from own textures in the theme background (with the background opacity setting) with a 1 px theme border, as wide as the health bar, overlapping it by 1 px so its bottom border and the health bar's top border are one line, and as tall as the name's font size plus 3 px above and below. Its top corner away from the portrait (right for player, pet and target of target, left for target and focus) is rounded with a 6 px radius: a quarter disc fill (`Media/corner-fill.tga`) and a 1 px arc (`Media/corner-line.tga`), mirrored through their TexCoords for the left corner. The fill pieces (body, top band short of the corner, quarter disc) never overlap, so the translucent fill stays even; the straight border lines stop where the arc starts. Each rect is read once per apply, before anything moves, so laying out again gives the same points; a Blizzard `SetPoint` / `SetAllPoints` on a laid-out bar or text (target classification, vehicle art) is answered by putting the layout back (hooked with `hooksecurefunc`). The bar fill's fixed-size mask is removed, and the value texts (`TextString`, `LeftText`, `RightText`) are anchored to the bar: centre, left +2 px, right -2 px. The value texts on both bars are 12 pt in Blizzard's font face (its size is too small to read) without its outline, which fills the digits in on a light fill, and with a 1 px black drop shadow (offset 1, -1). A region the client reports as protected is not moved in combat; combat end lays it out.
- Health and power values are never read. The unit frames' own points, size and every existing fade rule stay.

Restore: restore bar textures, colours, masks and points, the value text points, fonts and shadows, name (and its justification), level text font and points, skull points, hide the name strips, the player's portrait rim and the bar gloss, the player's, target's and focus portrait masks, crops and points, the combat icon size and points, release held art (portraits and level ring included) and hide own regions. The hooks stay installed but do nothing while the area is off.

The minimap, queue button (LFG eye), mail, tracking and the expansion landing page button remain visible and usable and are never faded. `GetMinimapShape()` returns `SQUARE`; the setup button projects onto the square edge. Restore: the container's latest Blizzard point the addon compartment's and day dial's points, exact original mask (the round `Textures\MinimapMask` when the client cannot read it), shape function or nil, wheel script/enabled state when it was changed, zoom-button mouse state and art alpha. The setup button returns to its circular placement. Hide own strip and border.

## Tooltips

Apply: themed background and a 1 px border on GameTooltip, item-reference and shopping tooltips. A tooltip with a `NineSlice` (all 12.0 tooltips) has that art held at alpha 0 and gets own regions; an older backdrop tooltip keeps `SetBackdropColor` / `SetBackdropBorderColor`. The unit health bar is held at alpha 0 (it hangs below the tooltip, so no gap is left); health values are never read. A player's name line takes the class colour (`RAID_CLASS_COLORS`) from a `TooltipDataProcessor` post call for units; a secret unit or class leaves Blizzard's colour. The border of an item follows its quality colour (`ITEM_QUALITY_COLORS`) from common up; a poor (grey) item and a tooltip shown with anything else get the theme border. Limit: the aura tooltip of the target frame (WoW Forever beta) lives in a frame that `EnumerateFrames` does not return and whose text no scan finds, so addons cannot reach it; it keeps Blizzard's border.

Scripts are never replaced on a `NineSlice` tooltip and `OnTooltipSetItem` is never touched (it throws on 12.0). The quality comes from one `TooltipDataProcessor.AddTooltipPostCall` for items (and the name colour from one for units), registered once when the addon loads, and a `Show` hook. Both do nothing while the area is off and ignore a tooltip the area did not skin. An old backdrop tooltip without a `NineSlice` gets exactly one `HookScript("OnShow")` for the session (a repeated apply adds none) that repaints the quality border while the area is on and does nothing once it is off. `SetScript` is never called on any tooltip and Blizzard's own `OnShow` keeps running.

Restore: original backdrop and border colours (old tooltips) and health-bar alpha; release the held `NineSlice`, hide own regions. The hooks stay but do nothing while off (no script is restored, none was replaced).

## Buffs and debuffs

Apply: square-crop the icons of the buttons in `BuffFrame.auraFrames` and `DebuffFrame.auraFrames` (temporary enchants are among them), add a thin border around the icon and outline the duration text beneath it. Older clients are found through `BuffButtonN`, `DebuffButtonN`, `TempEnchantN` and the children of the frames. Private aura anchors (`isAuraAnchor`) and forbidden or incomplete buttons are skipped. A debuff border takes the dispel colour of `button.buttonInfo.debuffType` from `AuraUtil.GetAuraBorderColor` (`DebuffTypeColor` on older clients, red for a debuff without a type); every other aura gets the theme border. Blizzard's `DebuffBorder` and `TempEnchantBorder` art is held at alpha 0. Aura fades, frame alpha, button size and layout stay and the frames are never shown or hidden.

New buttons and changed dispel types are skinned again after Blizzard's `UpdateAuraButtons` (hooked with `hooksecurefunc`) and on the player's `UNIT_AURA`. `UNIT_AURA` for any other unit does no work.

Restore: original crops, duration font and anchors, border art; own borders are hidden. The hooks stay but skin nothing while the area is off.

## XP and reputation

Apply: own 12 px XP bar centred on Blizzard's bar, so Blizzard's XP text sits on it, and a 4 px watched-reputation bar 2 px above it, both with a 1 px theme border, parented to Blizzard's status container (so existing fades still apply, and the bars never set their own alpha). The XP fill is blue with a vertical gradient (lighter at the top; flat where the client has no gradients) and a 1 px light edge along its top, on a dark theme-background track. Nine own dividers in the theme border colour split the bar into ten equal segments (twice as wide as Blizzard's twenty) and follow the bar width; rested XP is a translucent blue bar one frame level behind the XP fill covering the XP value up to `min(xp + exhaustion, maximum)`. Reputation reads `C_Reputation.GetWatchedFactionData()` (`GetWatchedFactionInfo()` on older clients) with `currentReactionThreshold`, `nextReactionThreshold` and `currentStanding`; nothing watched (no data, or `factionID` 0) hides the bar until a faction is watched. Blizzard's bar art is held at alpha 0: every texture of the container and of each tracking bar including nested frames (fill, segment ticks, exhaustion marker), looked at again on every refresh because Blizzard swaps tracking bars and fill textures; font strings are not held. Because Blizzard animates that art's alpha without going through `SetAlpha`, its vertex alpha is also set to 0 (colour kept; the old vertex colour comes back on undo). Blizzard's mouse behavior and text stay.

Entering the world and XP, exhaustion, level and faction events refresh the values; Blizzard art is looked at again on the next frame too, because Blizzard adds tracking bars and segment dividers in its own handlers after ours. Right after a reload the client drops alpha and vertex colour set on art whose atlas is still loading, so while some art still shows it is set again every 0.25 s, at most 40 times; nothing runs while the area is off. Ordinary gains ease out over 0.4 s and play a short glow at the end of the fill; at the same time the gained stretch flashes and fades over 0.8 s and 6 to 24 sparks (more for a bigger gain) rise from it and fade within about 1 s, clipped to the bar. The effect has its own per-frame script only while it plays, and restore stops it. Bubbles: 16 small soft dots (at most 60 % alpha) keep drifting left to right, 40 to 120 px over 2.5 to 4.5 s, and fading inside the filled part only (a clip frame ends at the fill texture). They are looping animation groups with no per-frame Lua; each loop moves a bubble to a random spot. They play while the bar is shown and stop when it hides or the area is restored. The rested bar follows at once; a lower value (level up) is set directly, and a new maximum applies even while a gain is animating. Secret values go directly to widgets; a secret XP, maximum or exhaustion skips the rested math and is never added or compared. Per-frame animation runs only while a gain is active and is removed afterwards.

Restore: stop animation, hide reusable own bars and release the stock art. Container alpha and points remain owned by Blizzard and Faders.

## Reset positions

While Modernize is checked, Reset positions clears character `windowPoints` immediately, independently of Save, and restores tracked original window positions. It stops dragging and waits for combat end before positioning when necessary, including after `/quiet off`. A window that is hidden when combat ends also gets Blizzard's point back, so it does not open displaced. Other settings remain. This explicit action differs from Reset default, which preserves saved positions.
