# Modern: Modernize

The Modern tab of `/quiet setup`. Modernize gives Blizzard's windows and frames a quiet dark look by skinning them in place. Skins change textures, colours, TexCoords, points of windows and their own child regions; fade rules (bars, player frame, XP bar, auras, party) stay alpha-only and are untouched, and a skin never hides a frame with `Hide()`, `Show()` or a state driver.

Modernize skins action bars, unit frames, cast bars, windows, map, minimap, tooltips, the damage meter, auras and XP/reputation. Each area can be enabled independently. Client frame variants are resolved at run time; missing parts are skipped. The actual look and client compatibility are checked in game.

## Settings

- Modernize is off unless the Modern tab checks it (`modernize.enabled`; missing means off). It is shared through presets and Reset default turns it off.
- While Modernize is checked the tab shows Theme, Background, Class colours and one checkbox per area. Unchecked, those options are hidden and keep their values. Nothing is stored until Save; closing the window discards the draft.
- Theme steps through the known themes. Today there is one: Dark (`modernize.theme`; missing or an unknown name means Dark).
- Background is the background alpha of skinned panels: 50, 75 or 90 percent (`modernize.opacity`; missing or any other value means 75). The stepper stops at both ends.
- Class colours is on unless unchecked (`modernize.classColors`; `false` turns it off). Areas that tint with the class colour read it.
- Area checkboxes follow the area registry in registration order, with each area's English label, in the right column. An area is on unless unchecked (`modernize.<area key>`; `false` turns that area off). Saving stores only values that differ from the defaults.
- The XP area (`status`, label Modern XP bar) heads its own XP and reputation section in the left column, under Reset positions. Under it, Show on big XP gain is on unless unchecked (`modernize.xpGain`; `false` turns it off) and is disabled while Modern XP bar is unchecked. While Modernize, the XP area and Show on big XP gain are on, quest XP and any single gain of at least 5 % of the level show the XP bar for 5 s (a further big gain extends it), even when the XP row is Only on hover. Mob-sized gains, a lower value after a level up and secret values do not; see [visible.md](visible.md).

## Theme

`dark` is background `0.05, 0.05, 0.05` with the chosen background alpha, opaque border `0.33, 0.33, 0.33, 1` (a translucent one is tinted by the world behind a translucent fill), accent `0.95, 0.75, 0.25` and the flat bar texture `Interface\Buttons\WHITE8X8`. A theme is one entry of data; `ns.Modern.Theme()` returns a copy of the active theme with the background alpha applied.

## Areas

An area is one file under `Features/Modern/Areas/` that calls `ns.Modern.Register{ key, label, apply, restore, events, onEvent, addons }`. A bad entry (missing key, label or apply, a reserved or duplicate key) is reported once and skipped.

- Apply: when Modernize and the area are on, `apply` runs once per area. Unchecking an area, or Modernize, restores only what changed. Changing the theme, background or class colours restores and applies the applied areas again.
- Restore: `/quiet off` or Reset restores every applied area in reverse registration order. Each area has a journal (`skin`) of what it changed; restore undoes it in reverse, so one area never touches another's frames, and held art alpha is released.
- Events: the events an area lists reach its `onEvent` only while the area is applied. Each event is registered on its own and a client that does not know it is skipped. `ADDON_LOADED` for an addon the area lists runs its `apply` again, so load-on-demand frames get skinned.
- Skin helpers: `Icon` (crop `0.08, 0.92, 0.08, 0.92`), `Border` and `Panel` (own regions, hidden on undo and reused), `Button` (flat fill, original art held hidden), `Hold` (art held at alpha 0, or a given alpha, against Blizzard through the alpha hook), and `Set` (any other `SetX` with its previous `GetX` value). A missing frame is skipped; a missing method is reported once.

## Movable windows

`ns.Modern.MakeMovable(frame, key)` adds an invisible drag handle over the top strip of a window. Dragging saves `windowPoints[key]` as `{ point, relativePoint, x, y }` relative to `UIParent` in the character settings (not in presets; Reset default keeps it). The saved point is put back on every show and after Blizzard positions its panels. In combat no point is set, no drag starts, and the wanted point is applied when combat ends. A window without a saved point keeps Blizzard's position. `ns.Modern.ReleaseMovable(frame)` stops the handle and returns to Blizzard's position; saved points are kept. The window's scripts are never replaced: one `HookScript("OnShow")` per window re-applies the saved point and does nothing once released; Reset positions re-anchors only windows that are still movable.

## Action bars

Apply: square-crop standard, multibar, pet, stance and gamepad action icons, replace button art with flat theme textures, add a thin border and outline hotkey/count text. Hold Blizzard bar decorations and slot art hidden: on 12.0 the `MainActionBar` `BorderArt` and the `EndCaps` (`LeftEndCap`, `RightEndCap`; the classic `MainMenuBar` textures as fallback), and per button `SlotArt`, `SlotBackground`, `NormalTexture` and `Border`. The 12.0 `IconMask` is taken off the icon so the square crop is not clipped. Keep the icon crop, button size, Edit Mode layout, bar alpha and combat behavior. Late gamepad loading receives the same skin through `ADDON_LOADED`.

State feedback: because Blizzard's hover, pressed and checked textures are held hidden, each button gets its own flat theme-coloured regions over the icon: a light hover region in the `HIGHLIGHT` layer, a dark one while pressed (`SetButtonState("PUSHED")` or mouse down) and an accent one while checked (`SetChecked`). They follow Blizzard through `hooksecurefunc` on `SetButtonState` and `SetChecked` and `HookScript` on `OnMouseDown` / `OnMouseUp`; no script is replaced, no field is written on the secure button, and nothing is shown or hidden on a bar. A secret checked state (12.0) is passed to the checked region with `SetAlphaFromBoolean` and never tested; a secret button state counts as not pushed.

Restore: release held art and restore button textures, crops, icon masks, fonts and colours; hide reusable own regions (including the state feedback). The hooks stay but do nothing while the area is off. The Bars feature exposes its buttons through `ns.Bars.Buttons()`.

## Unit frames

Apply: skin player, pet, target, target of target and focus in place. The retail 12.0 structure (`PlayerFrameContainer`, `TargetFrameContainer`, their content parts, `PetFrame` globals) is resolved at run time with the older flat names as fallbacks; missing or forbidden parts are skipped quietly.
- Frame art (frame texture, alternate power and vehicle textures, combat flash, resting glow `StatusTexture`, boss portrait frame, reputation colour strip) is held at alpha 0 against Blizzard's `SetAlpha`.
- Every unit frame is one compact bar, like a modern minimal HUD: one dark box (theme background with the background opacity setting) with a 1 px black border, which sinks into the box instead of outlining it, holds a near-black health bar and, along its bottom, a thin power line. Both bars get the theme flat texture and no border or gloss of their own; Blizzard retexturing a bar (`SetStatusBarTexture`) puts the theme texture back.
- Health is a quiet near-black (0.14, 0.15, 0.16) on every unit, so the white value text reads on it. It is reapplied after every Blizzard `SetStatusBarColor` / `SetStatusBarTexture`.
- Power colour follows the power type (`PowerBarColor`); mana is a softer blue (0.2, 0.45, 0.9) instead of Blizzard's pure blue. It is reapplied after `UnitFrameManaBar_UpdateType` (hooked with `hooksecurefunc`, no script replacement).
- Class colours on: the name of target, focus, pet and target of target takes the class colour for a player and the reaction colour (`FACTION_BAR_COLORS`) for an NPC. Off, or for a secret `UnitIsPlayer` / `UnitClass` / `UnitReaction` result (never used as a branch or table key), the name keeps Blizzard's colour, read once per apply. It is repainted on target and focus changes and on faction and classification events for the skinned units (events for nameplates or party members do no work).
- Portraits are held at alpha 0 in place (points, size and mask unchanged), also against 12.0 `SetAlphaFromBoolean`, which bypasses `SetAlpha` (every alpha hold re-applies its value after it). On a texture or font string the vertex colour's alpha is its alpha (Blizzard sets the target of target portrait's vertex colour every frame and colours the player's level text on level updates), so every alpha hold on one also re-applies its value after `SetVertexColor`, and on a font string after `SetTextColor`. The target's box border carries its rarity: gold for elite, silver for rare/rareelite, red for bosses; every other border is black.
- The box takes the portrait's space: it reaches from the portrait's outer edge (left for player, pet and target of target, right for target and focus) to the health bar's far edge and from the health bar's bottom 28 px up (18 px on pet and target of target). Inside its 1 px border the power bar is a 4 px line along the bottom, then a 1 px dark gap, and the health bar fills the rest; Blizzard's wider target power bar gets the same edges. The power value texts are held at alpha 0. The bar fill's fixed-size mask is removed, and the health value texts (`TextString`, `LeftText`, `RightText`) are anchored to the bar: centre, left +4 px, right -4 px, 12 pt in Blizzard's font face (its size is too small to read) without its outline and with a 1 px black drop shadow (offset 1, -1).
- Player: name and level text are held at alpha 0, with the textures anchored to the level text (its ring art), and so is the resting "zzz" (`PlayerFrameContent.PlayerFrameContentContextual.PlayerRestLoop`, classic `PlayerRestIcon` and `PlayerRestGlow`). The combat icon (`PlayerFrameContent.PlayerFrameContentContextual.AttackIcon`, classic `PlayerAttackIcon`) is 16 px and sits 3 px above the box's left end.
- Target and focus: a player unit shows its class icon (14 px, the cell of `CLASS_ICON_TCOORDS` on Blizzard's character-create class sheet, trimmed 0.02 at each edge) at the box's left end, 3 px above it, and the level follows 3 px right of it; an NPC, a secret identity or a client without `CLASS_ICON_TCOORDS` shows none and the level takes its place. It is repainted on every apply (target and focus changes, faction and classification events). The level text is a small number in the name's font (face and size; Blizzard's flags kept) at the box's left end, 3 px above it; its ring art is held at alpha 0 and the high-level skull is centred on it. The name follows 3 px right of the level, left-aligned, up to the box's right end. Pet and target of target have no level: their name spans the box the same way. Name and level sit above the box, not on it, because they belong to a frame drawn under the bars. Blizzard's own fonts are read once per apply, so applying again does not shrink them further.
- Each rect is read once per apply, before anything moves, so laying out again gives the same points; a Blizzard `SetPoint` / `SetAllPoints` on a laid-out bar or text (target classification, vehicle art) is answered by putting the layout back (hooked with `hooksecurefunc`). A region the client reports as protected is not moved in combat; combat end lays it out.
- Health and power values are never read. The unit frames' own points, size and every existing fade rule stay.

Restore: restore bar textures, colours, masks and points, the value text points, fonts and shadows, name (its justification and colour), level text font and points, skull points, the combat icon size and points, hide the bar box and its border, release held art (portraits, power value texts, the player's name, level and resting icon, level rings included) and hide own regions (the class icon included). The hooks stay installed but do nothing while the area is off.

## Cast bars

Apply: skin the cast bars of player, pet, target and focus in place (`PlayerCastingBarFrame`, classic `CastingBarFrame`, `PetCastingBarFrame`, `TargetFrameSpellBar`, `FocusFrameSpellBar`). Parts are the retail `CastingBarMixin` keys with the classic `<name><Part>` globals as fallbacks; a missing part is skipped. Nameplate cast bars are recycled across units and stay Blizzard's.
- Frame art (`Border`, `BorderShield`, `TextBorder`, `Background`, `Flash`, `ChannelShadow`, `StandardGlow`, `CraftGlow`, `InterruptGlow`, `Shine`) is held at alpha 0; because Blizzard animates the flash and glows past `SetAlpha`, their vertex alpha is 0 too (colour kept). The spark stays.
- The bar gets a theme panel behind it and a 1 px theme border. The fill takes the theme flat texture; Blizzard re-texturing it on every cast (`SetStatusBarTexture`) or recolouring it (`SetStatusBarColor`) is answered by putting the flat texture and the cast colour back (hooked with `hooksecurefunc`). The colour follows the retail `barType`: accent for a cast (standard, empowered, crafting), green (0.30, 0.75, 0.35) for a channel, grey (0.55) for an uninterruptible cast and the theme danger colour when interrupted. A client without `barType` keeps Blizzard's colours.
- Layout: the player's bar is 18 px tall, the pet, target and focus bars 14 px (width and points stay Blizzard's). The spell name sits inside the bar, left-aligned 4 px from its left end, and the cast time (`CastTimeText`) 4 px from its right end; both are outlined. Blizzard's `SetLook` (Edit Mode's look change) is answered by laying the bar out again (hooked with `hooksecurefunc`).
- Spell icon: Blizzard keeps the player's icon hidden but still sets its texture, so every bar gets an own square icon, as tall as the bar, 2 px left of it, cropped `0.08, 0.92, 0.08, 0.92` with a 1 px theme border. It mirrors the texture Blizzard sets on its `Icon` (`SetTexture` hooked; the current texture is copied on apply), and Blizzard's icon is held at alpha 0. A bar without a Blizzard icon shows an empty own icon.
- The bars' alpha and visibility stay Blizzard's.

Restore: restore the fill texture and colour, the bar height, the text points, justification and fonts, release held art (Blizzard's icon included) and its vertex colour, and hide the panel, borders and the own icon. The hooks stay but do nothing while the area is off.

## Reset positions

While Modernize is checked, Reset positions clears character `windowPoints` immediately, independently of Save, and restores tracked original window positions. It stops dragging and waits for combat end before positioning when necessary, including after `/quiet off`. A window that is hidden when combat ends also gets Blizzard's point back, so it does not open displaced. Other settings remain. This explicit action differs from Reset default, which preserves saved positions.
