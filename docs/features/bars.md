# Bars and Groups: action bars

Action bar fade groups, the swing timer, Enemy/Friend, and group visibility.

- Action bars fade in groups. A faded frame appears at once and fades out over 0.3 s. While every bar sits at its target alpha and nothing about which bars show changed, a pass skips the walk over the bar buttons; a full pass still runs at least every 0.2 s, so a button Blizzard shows on a faded bar fades with it. Hover shows the whole group from the bar rectangle, including empty slots. A filled button stays above that rectangle, so its tooltip and click still land on the spell. The default is bars 1–3 with the stance bar, the pet bar and the totem bar, bars 4–5, and each later bar on its own. The swing timer is group 10. The same number in `/quiet setup` fades together. Combat, a vehicle, edit mode, an instance (party, raid, pvp, arena), an open spell flyout, or an item on the cursor shows every group. Always visible in Group visibility keeps the entire selected group up, including the swing timer if it belongs to that group. Only on hover uses hover of any member to reveal the entire group. Enemy and Friend on a bar row keep that bar up while the target is alive and can be attacked (`UnitCanAttack`, and not `UnitIsDeadOrGhost`) or is friendly (`UnitIsFriend`), including out of combat. They do not show the rest of the fade group. A missing flag means off. The XP bar defaults to Only on hover, with Glance, Edit Mode, spell flyout and cursor-item exceptions. Unchecking Only on hover restores the usual bar triggers: combat, a vehicle, an instance, hover, Edit Mode, flyout and cursor items, plus 5 s after a quest turn-in that gives XP. Always visible keeps it up.
- Gamepad peek: while the gamepad bar would be faded, holding a trigger or shoulder button shows only the zone it drives, at once: LT the left zone (the LT bar and the left class button), RT the right zone (the RT bar, the stance bar drawn there and the right class button), both triggers or a shoulder button (LB, RB) the centre (the base bar, the LT+RT bar, the targeting and possess bars and the page tracker). The client sends no gamepad button events, so the pad state is read every 0.05 s while the gamepad bar is shown and not already forced visible. On release the bar fades out by the usual rules, the other zones staying hidden until it is gone. When the usual rules already show the bar, every zone shows. Alpha only; disabling releases the zone alphas.
